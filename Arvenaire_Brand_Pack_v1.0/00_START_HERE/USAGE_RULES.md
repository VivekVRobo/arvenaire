# Usage Rules

1. Use the **flat vector master** for normal identity work.
2. Use the **premium 3D mark** only at sizes where its material detail can survive.
3. Preserve the central opening and the two internal pathway cuts.
4. Do not redraw the mark as a literal letter A.
5. Do not add graduation caps, globes, planes, rockets, maps, or generic arrows to the master symbol.
6. Do not stretch, rotate, skew, outline, or apply drop shadows to the flat master.
7. Keep at least **25% of the mark's height** as clear space around the symbol.
8. Minimum recommended size: **16 px favicon; 24 px UI; 80 px for premium 3D use**.
9. Sapphire is an accent; keep it restrained. The master identity must remain legible without blue glow.
10. Primary light-ground mark: `#0B2444`. Primary dark-ground mark: `#F6F3EC` / platinum.
