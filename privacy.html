Privacy Policy

Effective and reviewed: 11 October 2026

This policy describes the information handled by ARVENAIRE's website and the external services linked from it. Please share only information needed for your request. Provider policies apply when you use their services.

1. Information submitted to ARVENAIRE

If you use the contact form, the form asks for your name, email address, target country, topic, message and preferred reply style. GPA/CGPA and a CV, LinkedIn, portfolio or GitHub link are optional. The message may include information you choose to add, such as your study or career goals and deadlines.

The resource-request form is currently paused; the Resources page does not ask for an email address to access a free PDF. An earlier site version may have saved an email in browser storage; the current contact page removes the legacy key scholar_leads if it remains in your browser.

We use contact-inquiry details to consider and, when accepted, respond to your request, and to support a service you book. The current website contact form is not a newsletter subscription.

2. Form service and retention

The current contact form submits data to Web3Forms, which processes form submissions and forwards them to ARVENAIRE. Its Privacy Policy, last updated 13 July 2026, says submissions may be retained for up to three years, with a shorter period possible under a plan setting or earlier deletion. The current retention setting for ARVENAIRE's contact form has not been verified. Please review the provider policy before submitting sensitive information.

ARVENAIRE keeps copies of inquiries only as needed to respond, provide support and meet applicable business or legal requirements. A specific retention period for copies forwarded to ARVENAIRE has not been set or verified. To request access, correction or deletion, use the contact form; applicable law and provider retention rules may affect the request.

3. Bookings and payments

Paid products and strategy-session bookings are linked to Topmate. Booking details and payment are handled through Topmate and the payment providers it identifies. The ARVENAIRE website forms do not ask for card or bank details. Review Topmate's Privacy Notice and the terms shown during booking before submitting information or paying. Topmate's notice explains its own data handling and communication practices.

4. Website requests and external services

The reviewed site source does not load an analytics or advertising-pixel library. Website hosting and security providers may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser and server-log data, under their own policies.

Pages may request typefaces from Google Fonts. That request sends standard network information, including your IP address, to Google so it can return the font. See Google Fonts' privacy explanation and Google's Privacy Policy.

This site also links to government, university, resource-hosting and social-media websites. When you follow an external link, the destination's privacy policy and terms apply.

5. Security and changes

We take reasonable care with inquiry information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. This policy may be revised when the site's forms, providers or business practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revision is made.
