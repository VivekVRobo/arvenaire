Career after study · South Korea

Make your Korea study plan a preparation plan for employment.

The study route can lead into employment preparation, but it needs active work: Korean-language progress, university career support, practical experience, and careful checking of the right stay and work requirements.

Use student support

Find opportunities through official channels

Study in Korea provides job-information platforms and employment support for international students. Use these alongside your university career office rather than relying only on social-media listings.
