Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

ARVENAIRE ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or purchase our educational products and consultation services.

1. Information We Collect

Information depends on the action you choose to take:

  • Contact-form data: Name, email address, target country, inquiry topic, reply preference, and the message you submit. GPA/CGPA and a CV, LinkedIn, portfolio, or GitHub URL are optional fields.
  • Free-resource request data: Resource downloads are available without an email. If you choose to submit an email in the download dialog, it is sent with the requested resource name through Web3Forms to process that request. The site does not intentionally persist those resource-request emails in browser local storage.
  • Payment & booking data: Paid product purchases and 1-on-1 bookings are handled through Topmate.io. Arvenaire does not receive or store your full card or bank credentials through the website forms.
  • Technical request data: Hosting and third-party service providers may process ordinary request or security data such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, and requested URLs as part of delivering their services. The current site should not be read as proof that a separate Arvenaire analytics programme is operating unless one is explicitly enabled.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide the resource, product, booking, or reply you requested.
  • Review the profile context you voluntarily submit and respond to scholarship, study, career, document, or portfolio questions.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website and its resources.
  • Maintain records where reasonably necessary for service delivery, support, dispute handling, or applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with trusted third-party service providers (such as Web3Forms for contact form processing and Topmate.io for checkout/scheduling) strictly to fulfill services requested by you.

4. Retention & Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the requested service, support, recordkeeping, dispute handling, or applicable legal obligations. Third-party providers may apply their own retention periods. We use reasonable technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Your Rights & Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by Arvenaire, subject to applicable obligations and records that may need to be retained. Use the Contact Page and state that your message is a privacy request so it can be identified correctly.
