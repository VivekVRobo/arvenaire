01 / Application logic

Start with the vacancy

Treat each application as a response to one real job description. The employer's live instructions decide which language, format and attachments to send. Germany's official guidance says applications commonly include a cover letter, CV and relevant certificates or references; employers may request upload or a PDF by email. Use the vacancy notice as the final checklist. [1]

A photo is not a universal rule

Official Germany guidance notes that photo expectations differ across industries. Follow the employer's instructions and your own choice; do not treat a photo as a legal requirement or a guarantee of selection. [1]

A five-step tailoring routine

  1. Decode: copy the role title, core tasks, required evidence, language, location and requested files into a vacancy note.
  2. Select: choose only truthful projects and experience that demonstrate those tasks.
  3. Translate: describe your contribution in the language requested by the employer. If the ad is in German, check whether German application documents are requested.
  4. Match: use the employer's task vocabulary where it accurately describes your experience; do not stuff keywords or claim tools you have not used.
  5. Check: compare the final files against the ad, filenames, requested format and dates.

Use a simple application log: employer, exact vacancy URL, role, date viewed, required language/files, CV version and next action. The log is for your own review, not a target count.

02 / Make evidence easy to scan

A practical CV structure

The German government portal describes the CV as typically tabular and lists personal/contact details, work experience, education, language skills, and relevant skills or interests. It also recommends reverse chronological order. Treat that as a useful starting point, then follow the vacancy and employer format. [1]

Format choices are employer-specific

Europass can help organize a profile and create a CV, but the German portal says to adapt the design to the company. Keep a clean editable master, then export the version requested by the employer. Do not assume one layout, page count or photo rule applies to every engineering vacancy. [1], [3]

03 / Engineering proof

Write project evidence, not adjectives

A strong project entry helps a reviewer answer four questions: what was the engineering problem, what did you personally do, how did you verify it, and what did the evidence show? Use a concise action statement followed by a result that you can substantiate.

A reusable sentence pattern

Problem or system + your action + method or tool actually used + verified output or learning. If there is no defensible metric, report the test, drawing, prototype, analysis or decision you completed.

Illustrative example — do not reuse unless it is true for you

Prototype example

Designed an encoder-based speed-monitoring prototype in Python, compared its output with bench readings, and documented where the signal became unreliable.

Make every number auditable. If you write that cycle time, error, energy use, cost or defect rate changed, keep the baseline, measurement method, test conditions and your role. If those details are confidential, remove them or describe the work at a level your employer permits.

Evidence that can support an engineering CV

  • A thesis or project abstract that names the question, method, result and limitation.
  • A drawing, simulation, control sequence, data notebook or test report that you are permitted to share.
  • A clearly scoped team contribution: your task, interfaces and decisions.
  • A compact portfolio index that links to approved, non-confidential artifacts.
Protect people, employers and research

Never upload proprietary drawings, customer data, production traces, credentials or unpublished lab material without permission. Replace sensitive details with a permitted synthetic example and label it synthetic.

04 / CV, portfolio and online presence

Make your professional profile consistent

A profile is a short index to evidence, not a second biography. Keep job titles, dates, degree names and skill descriptions consistent with your CV. Use a headline that names the engineering area you can support, not a seniority level you have not earned.

Recognition is a separate question

Whether formal recognition is required depends on the intended profession, title, qualification and route. Some engineering roles can involve a regulated professional title. Check the official Recognition Finder for the exact role and authority before making a claim about recognition. [2]

05 / Before you send

Final review and next actions

Spend your last review on accuracy and fit. The exact order of documents and application route comes from the vacancy notice. Keep source evidence ready for every claim.

  • The job title, employer name, location and reference number match the vacancy.
  • The first half of page one shows the evidence most relevant to this exact role.
  • Dates, degree names, tool names and language levels match your records.
  • Each achievement is true; every metric has a method and baseline or is removed.
  • The cover letter explains why this role and employer fit your real experience.
  • The requested language, attachment list, file type and submission portal are correct.
  • Links open; portfolio files are public only when approved; filenames are clear.
  • You saved the submitted version and recorded the actual submission date.
A useful post-application review

Record only what happened: confirmation received or missing, any follow-up instruction, interview feedback if offered, and the CV change you will make next time. One application outcome cannot establish a general employer preference.

Official starting points for finding roles

Use the official Make it in Germany listings as one search source and then verify the vacancy on the employer's own site. Search current task names and synonyms in the wording used by each employer. [4]

No hiring, interview, salary or visa outcome is promised by this guide. The content is general career information, not immigration, legal or recruitment advice. Verify the exact role, employer process and any regulated title with the responsible body.
